## Method

- Prefer Hellenistic, traditional, and medieval-derived technical frameworks over modern psychological astrology.
- Use Whole Sign Houses as the default house framework unless a document explicitly discusses another system.
- Use the seven traditional planets by default: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn.
- Use traditional rulerships only.
- Keep distinctions clear between Hellenistic sources, medieval Arabic transmission, later traditional usage, and modern commentary.
- Mark uncertain claims as uncertain.
- Do not invent source citations.

## Output Style

- Preserve the requested output format.
- Keep teaching material structured and reusable.
- Prefer clear definitions, procedural steps, examples, and comparison tables.
- Avoid excessive poetic or psychological language.
- Do not change existing calculation rules, scoring rules, or rectification logic from other parts of the project.
